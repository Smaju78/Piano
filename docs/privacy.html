Privacy

Piano is a free, personal, non-commercial website about classical piano music. It has no advertising and no analytics, accounts are optional, and unless you choose to sign in it does not collect personal information.

What is stored, and where

Your likes, the works you chose never to play, your jukebox filters, your theme choice, where you left off (page, work, recording and position) and a short list of recently played works are saved in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device, and you can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Unless you sign in, nothing is stored on a server.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, use the browser setting that clears site data for smaju78.github.io. The YouTube data this site keeps (video IDs, titles, channel names and view and like counts) is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days; videos that are removed from YouTube are dropped at the next refresh.

Sign in with Google (optional)

The whole site works without signing in. If you choose “Sign in with Google”, your likes, never-play list, jukebox filters, the “watch without ads” setting and where you left off (page, work, recording, position, programme) are kept in one document for your account in Google's Firebase service (Cloud Firestore), so they follow you to other devices. For sign-in, Firebase Authentication knows your Google account's name, email and photo; nothing else is collected, nothing is used for advertising or analytics, and nothing is shared. The security rules let only you read or change your document. Use “Delete my saved data” in the account menu to remove it at any time; to have the sign-in record removed too, open an issue.

YouTube

Recordings are played with the embedded YouTube player, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). When you play a video, YouTube and Google receive information as described in the Google Privacy Policy, and your use of the player is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. This site uses YouTube API Services to find recordings; that lookup runs offline, not in your browser.

While you are signed in with Google (unless you switch off “Watch without ads” in the account menu), the site uses the standard youtube.com player so that YouTube recognises your sign-in and Premium members see no ads. YouTube then uses its normal cookies in your browser. This site never sees or stores anything about your Google or YouTube account. The “Open in YouTube” buttons take you to youtube.com itself.

Other services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and video thumbnails from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Music data

Work details were compiled for this site and checked against Wikidata; movements come from MusicBrainz. Both are CC0 (public domain). Stored video details (titles, channels, view and like counts) are refreshed at least every 30 days.
